How each report is placed

  1. One id, one report. The ticket export pages overlap, so some reports appear twice. Count each id once.
  2. One-day move first. The desk’s notes list reports where the date moved by one day (oneDayMove in desk-notes.json). A day/month swap cannot move a date by one day, so these are their own problem, whatever the tenant.
  3. Then the tenant decides (causes.ts). Every tenant writes the day first. A report that came in through a booking-v3 form is hint: that form prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field, people type month first, and the form reads day first. Every other report is parser.

Intake’s tag is a guess from the reporter’s browser, so it is not used. The table shows how often it matched.

The problems

  • parser: the shared parser reads every date month first, so a day-first tenant’s 07/04 becomes 4 July. Fixed: “fix(dates): parse dd/mm for eu locales”.
  • hint: booking-v3 shows MM/DD/YYYY as the hint for day-first tenants and then reads the input day first. Won’t fix: the booking-v3 form is retired, so no new reports can come in this way.
  • one-day-move: the date shifts by one day (“Lisbon got Tuesday, Toronto got Monday”, “the invite arrived before I made the booking”). This points at time zones, not at day/month order. Still open: neither the parser fix nor retiring booking-v3 touches it.

What to change

  • Closing #4471 did not close every problem in it. Keep the one-day-move ticket open until someone finds the time-zone bug.
  • parser is closed as fixed and hint as won’t fix (linked in the table).
  • Read the body before you merge. Matching subjects grouped three bugs under one fix.
  • Re-read triage: duplicate reports; it has not been reviewed since June 2024.
